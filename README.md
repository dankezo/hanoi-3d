# Hanoi 3D

A pop-up paper Hanoi you can walk through. Flat paper tiles fold up ahead of you as you travel, and fold flat behind - a paper traveller with a conical hat (non la) wanders the streets, lakes and temples of Hanoi.

Fork of [xikhar/papertrails](https://github.com/xikhar/papertrails) (MIT licence), rebuilt as Hanoi.

[Play Hanoi 3D](https://dankezo.github.io/hanoi-3d/)

## The Hanoi map

Open [the Hanoi map](https://dankezo.github.io/hanoi-3d/?map=hanoi) (`?map=hanoi`) for a fixed 40x40 paper map of the city with 10 landmarks to discover: Hoan Kiem Lake, Ngoc Son Temple, West Lake, the Old Quarter, Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, Temple of Literature, One Pillar Pagoda, St. Joseph's Cathedral, Hanoi Opera House and Long Bien Bridge. Walk up to a landmark to check it off the list (top left), with a toast and a corner minimap. Without the flag you get the original endless procedurally generated world instead.

## How to play

- WASD / click to walk, Shift to run, Ctrl to slow down
- Tab for the atlas map, N for night, M for music, V to change view
- Touch: tap to walk
- No install, no account - open the link and walk

Credits: the endless pop-up paper world is [Papertrails](https://github.com/xikhar/papertrails) by Shikhar (@xikhar), MIT licence. The Hanoi map, its 14 landmark tiles, the conical-hat traveller and the discovery checklist are built on top of it in this repo.
